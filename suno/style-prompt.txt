Satirical Persian cabaret-punk: 1978 Tehran disco-funk meets California surf-thrash hardcore. Disco half (116 BPM, four-on-the-floor): walking slap bass under everything, clavinet jabs, wah chicken-scratch guitar, tight dead drums, harmonic-minor disco strings, muted horn stabs. Punk half (232 BPM): galloping bass, tremolo surf twang, spring reverb, thrash drums, still in the funk pocket. Dry, tape-saturated 70s mix, bass forward, sudden drops to bass and hi-hat, brass stops, rebuilds to full ensemble. Vocal: male Persian, manic high-tenor with wide nervous vibrato — sneering half-spoken asides, deadpan shrugs, gang-shout choir, one full-throat shriek, whispered punchlines, duck-squeak SFX. Structure: string intro, verse, bridge, breakdown, chorus, blaxploitation bass solo, accelerating four-on-the-floor climb, hard cut into closing thrash verse, climax chorus, gang-chant outro, tape-hiss fade. Mood: furious, sardonic, danceable, grinning through a black-comic hangover.
